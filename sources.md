## New work created for this portfolio

BridgePath Adult Learning is a fictional organization. The scenario, sample copy, budget, capacity limits and channel conversion rates were created for this independent exercise in November 2026. They are intended to be plausible assumptions that can be inspected and replaced; they are not research findings or industry benchmarks.

[scenario.json](data/scenario.json) contains the inputs. [analyze.py](analyze.py) computes the recommendation, alternatives and sensitivity results. The generated outputs report expectations rather than observed enrollments or historical client impact.

This work does not reproduce a historical PwC or Mercy deliverable, imply board approval, or claim endorsement by either organization. No client records, learner information, donor records or organizational logos are included.
